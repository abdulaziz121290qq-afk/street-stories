Designed and built this website together with Daniya: page layout, interactive elements, publishing and testing.

Key learnings

I work in marketing, so the concept of gastrodiplomacy was the most useful for me. Thailand’s government supported Thai restaurants abroad to shape how the world sees the country (Rockower, 2012), and Suntikul (2019) connects this directly to tourism. I had thought of a national cuisine as something that just exists. Appadurai (1988) showed that it can be built on purpose, through cookbooks in his case and through campaigns in Thailand’s.

This changed how I see food brands. Every brand that uses a country’s name or colours is making a claim about identity.

Personal motto

My guiding phraseA dish is a country’s best advertisement.

Most people will taste a country’s food before they ever visit it. What they taste becomes what they believe about the place.

Applying course themes

When I create advertising for food and drink, I now think about the culture behind the product and not only the product. I want to tell stories that are true to where something comes from, and I see how Uzbekistan could present its own street food to the world with more confidence.
