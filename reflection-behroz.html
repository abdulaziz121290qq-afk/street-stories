Behroz

Research · Part D

Wrote Part D with Saidakbar and Yakub: the Project Conclusion, Key Insights and the Discussion blog.

Key learnings

For the conclusion I had to decide what we really think about authenticity. I used to believe that there is one correct version of each dish. Johnston and Baumann (2010) describe authenticity as something people construct through stories about simplicity, history and place. Appadurai (1988) and Wilk (1999) both show national cuisines being assembled over time.

My blog post about café somsa came from this. I still prefer the tandir version, but I can no longer say that the new one is fake.

Personal motto

My guiding phraseAuthentic is what a community recognises as its own.

It moves the question from ingredients to people, which feels fairer and closer to how food actually changes.

Applying course themes

I will be slower to call food “fake” and more curious about why it changed. Writing a conclusion for thirteen people also taught me to find the common point in different opinions.
